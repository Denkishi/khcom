# Credits for the art in this folder

These sheets are fan-made and are not part of the original game. Their
authors ask to be credited wherever the sprites are used or edited.

- `extra_cards_1.png`, `extra_cards_2.png`: "Kingdom Hearts Chain of Memories
  More cards" (Extra Cards 01/02 and 02/02), from the Kingdom Hearts Customs
  section of The Spriters Resource. The author is not named on the sheets.

The card pictures the mod adds are cut from these sheets by
`tools/rogue_cards.py`.
