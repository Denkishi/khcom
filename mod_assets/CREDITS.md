# Credits for the art in this folder

These sheets are fan-made and are not part of the original game. Their
authors ask to be credited wherever the sprites are used or edited.

- `extra_cards_1.png`, `extra_cards_2.png`: "Kingdom Hearts Chain of Memories
  More cards" (Extra Cards 01/02 and 02/02), from the Kingdom Hearts Customs
  section of The Spriters Resource. The author is not named on the sheets.

- `station_of_calling.png`: "Station of Calling", 100% custom by Windlord
  (credit asked on the sheet), from the same section.

- `prizes.png`: "Kingdom Hearts Chain of Memories Prizes Sprites" (Prizes &
  Drops), from the same section. The author is not named on the sheet.

The reward icons are cut from it by `tools/rogue_cards.py`. The hub's
background is made from the Station of Calling sheet by `tools/rogue_backgrounds.py`. The
card pictures the mod adds are cut from these sheets by
`tools/rogue_cards.py`.
- `mickey_kh2.png`: "KH2FM Secret Ending Mickey Mouse", edited by Roxas of KHE and KH Sprites, original mugs and sprites ripped by Bacon. The sheet asks for credit and not to be claimed as one's own.
- `roxas_sheet.png`: "The Ultimate Roxas Sprite Sheet", a custom sheet by Bitsverse644; the sheet credits its sprites to White-wolf8, CNA, Skyrio, TheKCroxas, bybyblue, likeandhate, RodgrioKunGames, Bowser-The-Second, omegaslasgerdude and Lonesomeazn.
- `sephiroth_ffrk.png`: Sephiroth (Kingdom Hearts II boss) from Final Fantasy Record Keeper, as ripped for The Spriters Resource.
- `sephiroth_mgssj2.png`: "MGSSJ2 Sephiroth Sprite Sheet", a custom sheet by MGSSJ2, as the sheet names itself.
- `sora_kh2_ultimate.png`: "Sora From The Kingdom Hearts Series Ultimate Sprite Sheet", a custom sheet in the style of Chain of Memories, as the sheet names itself.
- `zenaki_dump_1.png`, `zenaki_dump_2.png`: standing sprites of Kingdom Hearts characters by Zenaki, as the files were named when they were supplied.
