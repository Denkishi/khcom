# A combo finisher launches the enemy it hits. Reads the height of the enemy
# for a second and a half after a plain swing made a finisher.
# Expect: the lowest z printed is far below 0 (about -22000); without the
# poke it stays 0.
include battle.txt
poke8 @gRogue.finisher 1
debug attack 12
wait 20
peek32 @gRogueDebug.targetZ z_after_20
wait 20
peek32 @gRogueDebug.targetZ z_after_40
wait 20
peek32 @gRogueDebug.targetZ z_after_60
peek16 @gRogueDebug.hits hits
