# Doors open with the game's own map cards, on its own screen ("Sintesi
# della stanza"): any card opens any door, whatever its number, and the room
# behind is the card's. A run starts with a plain battle's card and a rest's,
# every battle leaves another, and a door struck with none in hand gives a
# plain battle's. Here the first room's door is struck (by a test switch)
# and the second card, the rest's, is played on it.
# Expect: the first picture shows the game's card screen with the two cards
# and the second the rest's card ("Tregua Sospirata") under the glove; the
# third Sora holding the card up to the door; the fourth the next room.
# kind reads 5 (a rest room), used reads 1, room reads 1, the counter runs.
include start.txt
poke8 @gRogueDebug.strike 1
wait 200
shot /tmp/cards_0.ppm
press RIGHT
wait 20
shot /tmp/cards_1.ppm
press A
wait 40
press A
wait 60
shot /tmp/cards_2.ppm
wait 300
hold B 100
wait 100
shot /tmp/cards_3.ppm
peek8 @gRogue.kind kind
peek8 @gRogueDebug.mapUsed used
peek8 @gRogue.room room
peek32 @gFrameCounter f1
wait 30
peek32 @gFrameCounter f2
