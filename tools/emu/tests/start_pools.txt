# The deck a run starts with grows with what the player has done. A new save
# draws from a few plain cards numbered 1 to 4; with three chapters open and
# three runs won the pool is wide, and the collection gets a spread of spare
# cards to make the deck over with from the pause menu.
# Expect, for the new save: stage 0, high 4 or less, spare 0. Poke chapters
# and wins to 3 before the first START for the other: stage 3, high 6 or
# less, spare above 20.
wait 600
press START
wait 120
press START
wait 200
hold B 100
wait 20
peek8 @gRogueDebug.startStage stage
peek8 @gRogueDebug.startHigh high
peek8 @gRogueDebug.startSpare spare
peek32 @gFrameCounter frames
